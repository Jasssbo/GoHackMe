GoHackMe

Privacy Policy  |  Last updated: July 2025
GDPR COMPLIANT

— 1. WHO WE ARE —

GoHackMe is an open-source mobile game developed by Jasssbo. The source code is available at github.com/Jasssbo/GoHackMe_Flutter.

For data-related enquiries, contact us by opening an issue at github.com/Jasssbo/GoHackMe_Flutter/issues.

— 2. WHAT WE COLLECT —

GoHackMe is built to collect the absolute minimum. Here is everything we process:

DataWhere storedRetentionThird party?
Display name (entered by you)Your device only (SharedPreferences)Until you clear app dataNo
Name history (last used names)Your device only (SharedPreferences)Until you clear app dataNo
Country (derived from IP)Server RAM, game room onlyMax 2 hoursVia ipapi.co *
Chat messagesServer RAM, game room onlyMax 2 hoursNo

* We send your IP to ipapi.co to determine your country code. Only the country code is retained by us — your IP is never stored. The coordinate is replaced with a fixed national centroid (GDPR Art. 5(1)(c) — data minimisation).

— 3. WHY WE COLLECT IT —

  • Display name: to identify you to other players in your game room. Legal basis: contract / legitimate interest.
  • Country: to display your country flag on the lobby globe widget. Legal basis: legitimate interest.
  • Chat messages: to enable real-time in-game communication. Legal basis: contract.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the game.

— 4. WHAT WE DON'T COLLECT —

  • No account registration, no email, no password.
  • No precise location data.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs (no Firebase, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude).
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID).
  • No persistent server-side database — all server data is in RAM only.
  • Fonts are bundled locally — no Google Fonts CDN requests.

— 5. DATA SHARING —

We share data with one external service:

  • ipapi.co — receives your IP address to return a country code. Their privacy policy is available at the link above.

The game server runs on Render.com (Oregon, USA), which participates in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. No game data is stored to disk — Render has no access to persistent user data.

— 6. YOUR RIGHTS (EU / GDPR) —

If you are in the EU or EEA, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Lodge a complaint — with your national supervisory authority (e.g. CNIL in France, BfDI in Germany).

To exercise any of these rights, open an issue at our GitHub repository.

— 7. HOW TO DELETE YOUR DATA —

  • Local data (display name, name history): Settings → Clear local data in the app, or uninstall the app.
  • Server data (country, chat): automatically deleted when the game room closes — maximum 2 hours after the last player disconnects.

— 8. CHILDREN —

GoHackMe is rated 12+. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (US) or under 16 (EU). If you become aware that a child under these ages has provided data through the app, please contact us via GitHub Issues so we can take appropriate action.

— 9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY —

We may update this policy when our data practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

— 10. CONTACT —
